# User personas

## 1. Thando — first salary

**Age:** 22 · **Location:** Soweto, Gauteng · **Income:** R8,000/month (entry-level job)

**Context:** Thando started her first job. Rent is R2,500, transport R1,200, and her
mother expects R500 each month. Friends want to go out; there is a phone contract
she is considering.

**Goals**

- Know exactly what is left after the month's obligations
- Start an emergency fund without feeling broke
- Stop being surprised by month-end

**Frustrations**

- "My money disappears by the 20th and I cannot explain why"
- Bank apps show balances but not understanding
- Financial advice online is written for a different economy

**MoneyWise SA jobs**

- Dashboard for the reality check, Budget for overspend warnings
- Savings goal: Emergency fund, R15,000 target
- Assistant question: "Can I afford a R1,500 phone contract?"

## 2. Sipho — student on allowance

**Age:** 20 · **Location:** Durban, KZN · **Income:** R3,500/month (NSFAS allowance + weekend work)

**Context:** Money arrives in instalments and must last the full period. Accommodation,
food, data and airtime compete with social pressure.

**Goals**

- Stretch the allowance to the end of the period
- Save for a laptop without borrowing
- Understand whether a student overdraft is worth it

**Frustrations**

- Budgets that assume a stable monthly salary
- Feeling pressured to spend when friends spend
- Not knowing what credit will do to his record

**MoneyWise SA jobs**

- Income source: "NSFAS or student allowance"
- Calculators: savings projection for the laptop, 50/30/20 split
- Learning hub: Credit and interest, Spotting financial scams

## 3. Naledi — gig and informal income

**Age:** 27 · **Location:** Cape Town · **Income:** variable, ~R6,000–R9,000/month (hairdressing and freelance gigs)

**Context:** Income fluctuates. Some clients pay late. She supports two siblings at
school and contributes to a stokvel.

**Goals**

- Separate business money from personal money
- Build a buffer for slow months
- Understand tax and VAT thresholds before scaling up

**Frustrations**

- Cannot answer "can I afford this?" when income varies
- Stokvel commitments are hard to plan around
- Irregular income makes standard budgets useless

**MoneyWise SA jobs**

- Budget categories: School fees, Stokvel, Family support, Health
- Emergency fund calculator sized on essentials, not income
- Learning hub: Loans and repayments, Investing basics

---

## 4. Bongani — supporting family on a tight budget

**Age:** 29 · **Location:** Ekurhuleni · **Income:** R12,000/month (factory salary)

**Context:** Sends money home monthly, pays a furniture-store instalment and a
short-term loan. Wants to clear debt but keeps missing the bigger picture.

**Goals**

- Get debt repayments under control
- Understand total cost, not just instalments
- Stop using one credit product to pay another

**Frustrations**

- Instalments look affordable until interest is counted
- Fees he did not expect
- No clear picture of what to pay off first

**MoneyWise SA jobs**

- Debt repayment calculator (balance, instalment, interest rate)
- Money Confidence Score: Debt management pillar as the focus
- Learning hub: Credit and interest, Loans and repayments
